# Blocks

The theme comes with ready-made blocks for the parts of a restaurant, tavern or inn site: hero, menu, hours, locations, events, rooms, gallery, reviews and more. You add them in the page editor with the **+** button, like any block.

## Choose which blocks you see

Open **Settings → Theme Settings → Blocks**. Blocks are grouped by what they do (Page sections, Menus and food, Locations and hours, Events, Rooms and stays, Gallery, Journal and newsletter, Site frame). Each group is a tile with the number of blocks under its name: pick a tile to see only that group, or **All** to see everything. The address changes with the tile, so you can bookmark or share a group. The arrow keys, Home and End move between tiles.

- **Tick** a block to keep it in the block picker.
- **Un-tick** a block to hide it from the picker. Use this to keep the list short for the people who edit your pages.
- **Save blocks** applies your choice. **Show all blocks** turns everything back on.

Hiding a block only removes it from the picker. A block already on a page keeps working and stays editable, and the page layouts (patterns) that include it still insert. Nothing on your live site changes. New blocks that arrive with theme updates start switched on.

A few blocks are not listed because they only work inside templates and never appear in the picker: Event Details, Room Details and Site Footer.

## The Journal and newsletter blocks

- **Journal**: your blog page (featured post, search, categories, sorting, a grid and page numbers). It is used on the Journal page and on category, tag and search pages.
- **Journal Post**: one post, with its byline, contents list, sharing, newsletter form, author and related posts. It is used in the single post template.
- **Latest from the Journal**: the newest posts as cards. Good on the home page and the Story page.
- **Press and Diner Quotes**: quote cards with stars from the posts in a category (In the press, Diner write-ups).
- **Newsletter Sign-up**: your newsletter form, in a band, on any page. What it says and where it sends addresses is set under Settings → Theme Settings → Newsletter.

## Where each block gets its content

Most blocks read your locations, menus, events and rooms, so you edit that content under their own menus rather than inside the block. See [Locations & hours](locations-and-hours.md), [Menus](menus.md), [Events & private dining](events.md) and [Rooms & stays](rooms-and-stays.md).
