# Branding & styles

## Logo and brand

**Settings → Theme Settings → Brand**: upload a logo (SVG or PNG, about 120 px tall), set the brand line (“Dining rooms”, “Tavern & kitchen”) and the year established. Without a logo, the crest uses your first initial and year.

## Four styles

| Style | Mood |
| --- | --- |
| **Lampwright** | Candlelit old town: lamp-black and brass, Bodoni Moda |
| **Ember & Arch** | Brick and firelight: deep red-brown and ember orange, EB Garamond |
| **Ashlar & Iron** | Limestone and iron: pale stone and claret, Marcellus |
| **Daylight** | Café and brunch: warm white, terracotta and sage, Outfit |

Switch in the setup wizard (*Look*) or **Appearance → Editor → Styles**. A style changes colours, fonts, textures and the placeholder illustrations together. Switching replaces colour/font changes you made in the Site Editor; earlier versions stay under Styles → Revisions.

## Demo switchers

Importing the demo content adds two switchers to the header so visitors can compare the three kinds of home page (Restaurant, Tavern, Bed & Breakfast) and the four styles. They are for showing the theme off, not for a live site. To remove them, edit the header in **Appearance → Editor**, select the **Site Header** block and turn off **Show demo switchers (style and business type)** in its settings.

### The demo strip

Under the demo bar is a second strip that says **Demo content included** and introduces you: a small round photo or logo, a one-line tagline (up to 120 characters) and a button such as “Hire me” (text up to 30 characters, with its own link, opening in a new tab). Fill them in under **Settings → Theme Settings → Brand → Demo strip**. Leave a field empty to leave that part out; the strip still says demo content is included. It hides together with the demo bar, so it never shows on a live site.

## Editing pages

Pages are built from blocks. **Appearance → Editor** edits templates (header, footer, the 404 page); each page’s content is edited from **Pages**. Cobble & Candle blocks are in the inserter under **Cobble & Candle**: Hero, Hours strip, Signature dishes, Menu teaser, Full menu, Story, Timeline, Values, Reviews, Upcoming events, Rooms, Gallery, Private dining, Reservations, Locations explorer, Contact, CTA band and more. Click a block to change its text in the sidebar.

## Photos

Placeholder illustrations show until you add a photo, so nothing looks broken while you gather pictures. Use landscape photos for page headers; the dark overlay keeps text readable.
