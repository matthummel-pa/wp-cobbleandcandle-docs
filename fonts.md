# Fonts

Open **Settings → Theme Settings → Fonts** to use fonts from the [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) library for your headings and your text. Each style of the theme comes with its own fonts. Anything you choose here replaces them everywhere on your site and in the page editor.

## Your visitors never contact Google

When you add a font, **your own site downloads it once** and keeps the files in its uploads folder. From then on your pages are served the files from your own site. Nothing is requested from Google while someone reads your site, which keeps visitors' details private and avoids cookie-banner questions about Google Fonts. The only time anything contacts Google is while you are on this tab: the font list and the little sample pictures come from WordPress.org.

## Add a font

1. Under **Add a font from Google Fonts**, type a name (for example *Lora* or *Playfair*) and choose **Search**. You can narrow by kind: serif, sans serif, display, handwriting or monospace. With no search, a few popular choices are shown.
2. Tick the styles you need. **Regular and Bold** are ticked for you. **Also add italics** adds the italic version of each tick.
3. Choose **Add this font**. It takes a few seconds.

You can keep up to six fonts and up to six styles of each one.

## Choose where to use it

Under **Where to use your fonts**:

- **Headings** are page titles and the big lines on every page. You can also pick the heading weight (for example Bold).
- **Text** is paragraphs, buttons, menus and forms. Add Regular **and** Bold, so bold text uses a real bold font instead of one the browser makes up.

Choose **Save fonts**, then reload your site. **Go back to the theme's fonts** switches everything back at once.

## Keep it light

Fonts are among the heaviest things on a page. The aim is **120 KB or less** for the fonts a page uses. The tab shows the total for the fonts you have chosen. Sizes vary a lot between fonts: some are about 45 KB a style, and some are over 100 KB a style. A font that is over 100 KB on its own is marked **Large**. To keep pages quick:

- Use **one** font for headings and **one** for text, or the same font for both.
- Add only the styles you use. Regular alone is often enough for headings.
- Choose a lighter font if a page feels slow.

## Remove a font

Under **Fonts on your site**, choose **Remove** next to a font. Its files are deleted from your site, and anything that used it goes back to the theme's font. Removing the plugin with **Remove data on uninstall** ticked also deletes your fonts.

## Good to know

- If a font cannot load, visitors see a similar standard font for a moment, so the page stays readable.
- Italic text is only slanted when the font has an italic style. A heading font with no italic added shows upright letters wherever the theme would have used italics.
- The fonts are open-licensed, and the list is the one WordPress's own Site Editor offers.
