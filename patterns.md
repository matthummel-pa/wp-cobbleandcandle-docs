# Patterns and page layouts

Patterns are ready-made sections you drop into any page. In the editor choose **Add block (+) → Patterns → Cobble & Candle**, or search for the name. Everything in a pattern is normal blocks, so change the words, photos and colours freely.

## Home pages

Three home pages ship with the theme, one per kind of business. The setup wizard and the demo import use them.

| Pattern | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | A restaurant: hero, hours strip, menu teaser, chef’s picks, story, events, rooms, private dining, reviews and a photo mosaic |
| **Home — Tavern** | A tavern or pub: ale, long tables, events |
| **Home — Bed & Breakfast** | An inn: rooms first. The hero and the rooms section are **locked**: you can edit their text and images, but not move or remove them, so a page can’t be broken by accident |

On the demo pages the header’s main button follows the kind of business: the Bed & Breakfast home page offers **Book a stay** instead of a table request.

## Page layouts

| Pattern | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu page** | The full menu with diet filters |
| **Reservations page** | Table requests or your booking widget |
| **Gallery page** | Photos with a lightbox |
| **About page** | Your story, timeline and values |
| **No results** | The message shown when a search or filter finds nothing |

## Sections for inns and B&Bs

Add these anywhere, most often to the **Rooms** page.

| Pattern | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **What comes with the key** | What a stay includes |
| **Good to know (rates & policies)** | A policies table: check-in and check-out, cancellation, children, pets, parking, breakfast |
| **Stay & dine call to action** | A prompt to book a room and dinner together |
| **Around the house (area guide)** | Places to eat, walk and visit nearby |
| **Questions guests ask** | FAQ rows that open and close. Built from WordPress’s core Details block, so no plugin is needed |

The **Rooms** archive already shows what a stay includes, a policies table and the stay and dine prompt under your rooms.

## Tips

- Replace the sample text before you publish. Placeholder copy is written to show the layout, not to describe your place.
- Patterns follow your chosen [style](branding-and-styles.md), so switching style restyles them.
- A pattern is copied into the page when you insert it. Later theme updates don’t change pages you already built.
