# SEO

Cobble & Candle tells search engines exactly what you are, without extra plugins:

| Page | What Google gets |
| --- | --- |
| Every page | Organization, WebSite, breadcrumbs, canonical URL |
| Home, Locations | **Restaurant** per house: address, map pin, hours including holidays, cuisine, price range, phone, reservations and order links |
| Menu | **Menu** → sections → dishes with prices and diet tags |
| Event pages | **Event**: date, place, price, availability |
| Room pages | **HotelRoom** with occupancy, beds and nightly **Offer** |
| Journal posts | **BlogPosting** with author, publisher, photos, word count, reading time and keywords, plus breadcrumbs (Home, Journal, category, post) |

With **Yoast SEO** or **Rank Math** active, these pieces join their graph instead of being printed twice. Without an SEO plugin, the theme prints a meta description, Open Graph and Twitter tags (page excerpt → description; featured image → share image).

## Help Google help you

- Fill **cuisine** and **price range** in Settings → Theme Settings → Brand.
- Give each page an **excerpt** (Page → Excerpt panel); it becomes the description.
- Keep holiday hours up to date: Google shows them in your listing.
- Write a short **search title** and **description** for each journal post (the *Search and sharing* panel in the post editor), and use clear headings: see [The Journal](journal.md#search-engines-and-sharing).
- Claim your **Google Business Profile** and link it under Settings → Theme Settings → Social & sharing.
