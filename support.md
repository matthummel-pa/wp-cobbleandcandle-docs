# Support

Cobble & Candle is a WordPress block theme for restaurants, taverns and inns, with a companion plugin (**Cobble & Candle Core**) that holds your locations, menus, events, rooms, bookings and messages.

## Get answers fast

Most questions are answered in the [guides](README.md). The [FAQ](faq.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) cover the common ones.

## Before you write to us

1. Open **Tools → Cobble & Candle status**. Fix anything marked **Problem** or **Check**; most issues (emails not arriving, pages 404ing, wrong times) show up there.
2. Click **Send a test email to me**. If it fails, install an SMTP plugin such as [WP Mail SMTP](https://wordpress.org/plugins/wp-mail-smtp/). Guests’ requests are still saved under **Messages**.
3. If you use a page cache (LiteSpeed, WP Rocket, your host’s cache), purge it.
4. Confirm both the theme **Cobble & Candle** and the plugin **Cobble & Candle Core** are active and up to date.
5. Under **Settings → Permalinks** choose “Post name”, and under **Settings → General** set a city timezone.

## Contact support

Email **hello@matthummel.com** and include:

- What you did, what you expected, what happened (screenshots help).
- The **system report** from Tools → Cobble & Candle status (copy and paste it). It contains versions and settings, never passwords, emails or guest details.
- The page URL, and the browser and device if it is a display issue.

Please do not send guest names, emails, phone numbers or passwords.

Purchases, licences and refunds go through the store where you bought the theme. An active licence includes updates and support; if it expires, the theme keeps working.

## Security

Found a vulnerability? Email **hello@matthummel.com** with “Security” in the subject. Please do not post it publicly. Include the affected version, the steps to reproduce and the impact (for example, which user role is needed).

## What Cobble & Candle does not do

- Take card payments for rooms or tables (every stay and table is a request you confirm).
- Replace a full point-of-sale or online-ordering system (link your Toast, Square or ChowNow ordering page instead).
- Guarantee legal accessibility compliance. We test against WCAG 2.2 AA, but your content matters too.
- Require Elementor, ACF or any page builder.
